Welcome Guide: Discover Getxotri

How the club works, what it offers, and what it means to be part of our team.

GU GARA GETXOTRI
Who We Are
A club to experience triathlon in many ways

The main goal is to promote and facilitate triathlon practice. We are open to diverse backgrounds, athletic ages, fitness levels, and goals: from beginners to high-performance competitors.

Sprint Middle Distance Long Distance Duathlon Teams
Core Principle
Athletic freedom, collective commitment

Athletic freedom: Each person decides how they train, how much they race, which distances they practice, and what their goals are.

Collective commitment: Individual freedom coexists with responsibilities needed to sustain the shared project.

💡 Belonging to Getxotri does not depend on race results, pace, distances, or number of competitions.
Club Values
  • 01 Sport: Amateur, but with rigor and discipline.
  • 02 Respect: Camaraderie, rules, and diversity.
  • 03 Participation: Your help and enthusiasm count.
  • 04 Atmosphere: We train and enjoy together.
  • 05 Education: We learn with and from sports.

Club Identity & Pride
Getxo Triathlon – Agustín Ugarte Memorial

The Basque triathlon circuit exists thanks to local clubs dedicating time, effort, and resources so everyone can compete. Organising the Memorial is our way of giving back to the community.

01 BEFORE

Planning, permits, communication, and setup tasks.

02 DURING

Volunteering, course safety, athlete assistance, and support.

03 AFTER

Teardown, closing, evaluation, and improvements for next year.

Joining the club means contributing to the Memorial

Whether competing or not, every member's collaboration in event organisation or volunteering is a core pillar of our team identity.
